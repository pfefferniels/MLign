## 5. Checkpoint selection: the lesson

Three selection criteria picked three different checkpoints of the same run (v5real):

| criterion | picks | holdout match F |
|---|---|---|

| Vienna 4x22 dev record | epoch 5 | 0.9799 |
| mixed synthetic+real val loss | epoch 29 | (not run — known-invalid) |
| **real-music val loss** (`realgt-val`) | epoch 21–23 | **0.9878** |

In-run measurement (v5real log): dedicated real val loss bottoms at epoch 22; the mixed
criterion keeps improving monotonically to epoch 29 — the two criteria part company at
epoch 23. `eval/run_devlong.py` (20 long, deletion-heavy train-split sonata movements)
was calibrated to rank checkpoints like the holdout does (v3 > v5-e5), where 4x22 ranked
them backwards; it is the primary local selector going forward.
